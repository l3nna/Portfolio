// SYSTEM_OVERVIEW

BUILT FOR
WEIGHTY MOVEMENT

RE:SHIFT is a character locomotion system designed to combine responsive controls with grounded and believable animation. The system uses animation warping, inverse kinematics, aim offsets, and character state logic to create movement that feels connected to the character rather than simply moving a model through the environment.

// PERSPECTIVE_SYSTEM

FPS ↔ TPS

The system supports switching between first-person and third-person perspectives while maintaining consistent character movement and animation behaviour.

Camera logic, character rotation, animation states and locomotion behaviour are synchronized to provide a seamless transition between perspectives.

// CORE_FEATURES

SYSTEM COMPONENTS

01

8-WAY LOCOMOTION

Full directional movement with smooth animation blending across forward, backward, strafing and diagonal movement.

02

STRIDE WARPING

Dynamic animation adjustment that allows movement animations to adapt to different speeds and distances.

03

ORIENTATION WARPING

Adjusts animation orientation to match the desired movement direction while maintaining natural motion.

04

FOOT PLACEMENT

Advanced IK-based foot placement keeps the character's feet aligned with uneven terrain.

05

CHARACTER LEAN

Subtle procedural leaning adds weight and physicality to directional movement.

06

AIM OFFSET

Upper-body aiming operates independently from the lower-body locomotion system.

// TECHNICAL_IMPLEMENTATION

ANIMATION
PIPELINE

01

ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

Character movement states are processed through an animation blueprint that controls locomotion, aiming, leaning and perspective-specific behaviour.

02

IK & FOOT PLACEMENT

Procedural inverse kinematics are used to maintain stable foot contact with different surfaces.

03

ANIMATION WARPING

Orientation and stride warping dynamically modify animations according to movement direction and speed.

04

CAMERA STATES

First-person and third-person camera states are integrated with the character's locomotion logic.
